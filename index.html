⚙ GEAR MILL

Link the windmill drive gear to the mill with a gear train.

  • Tap a square to raise/retract it (its pin goes with it). A square only rises if its 4 corner posts are up.
  • Tap near a corner to toggle a post. Dropping a post drops the squares touching it.
  • Gears roll along the top rack and tumble down the slope. They seat on raised pins they pass.
  • Seated gears touching side-by-side mesh. Connected to the windmill = turning = +1 pt/sec each.
  • Glowing power gears keep spinning (and powering neighbours) 15 s after disconnect.
  • More pins up = gears roll in faster. 50 gears per game. Hook up the mill for a bonus!
