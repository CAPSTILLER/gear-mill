⚙ GEAR MILL

Link the windmill drive gear (it starts at the bottom) up to the mill (at the top) with a gear train.

  • Tap a square to raise/lower its center pin. Tap a border post (outside ring) to toggle it.
  • A square lifts by itself when the 4 pins/posts beside it (left, right, up, down) are all up. It drops if any of them drops.
  • Gears roll along the top rack and tumble down the slope. Each gear has an open U-slot (gold-edged) and a solid half. If the slot hits a raised pin or yellow border post, the gear hooks on. Hit with the solid half and it bounces off.
  • A hooked gear falls off when its slot turns to face up, unless a gear or raised square is right below it holding it. Spinning gears swing past slot-up, and unpowered gears sag that way, so give them support.
  • A gear hooked on a border post is locked on and won't fall. Tap that post once to drop the gear, then tap it again to toggle the post.
  • If a border gear turns, that whole side spins: each post turns the opposite way to the one next to it, and any gear on those posts turns too and can power its own train.
  • JAM: two drivers (windmills, spinning sides, power gears) that force opposite directions on one train lock the whole train, shown with a red ✕. It frees up when the gear causing the clash is removed.
  • Each border post is paired with the post straight across the board. When a pair goes from different to matching (the second one goes up to match, or down to match), that whole row or column flips. The post that doesn't match yet pulses.
  • Pull the levers in the top-left and bottom-left corners to toggle that whole border row at once. They're free, with a 1 s cooldown. The lever boxes are solid, so gears bounce off them.
  • Gears never overlap. They bounce off each other, and a gear won't hook onto a pin if there's no room.
  • ◎ HOLE GEARS (100 gears, from Queue first, then Credit, then Ante): for 100 s gears have no slot. They hook onto any raised pin they touch and stay on.
  • Every 2 min a SWEEP runs clockwise from the top-left post. It toggles one post per second, twice round the board.
  • Gears mesh side by side on the same level (floor or lifted). Gears on border posts count as floor level.
  • The windmill has a floor gear and an upper gear. Turning gears score +1 pt/sec each.
  • Every gear you newly connect earns 10 gears back from the bin (credit pays out as gears hit the bin).
  • Link a lifted gear to a windmill's upper gear: the whole bin returns to the queue and a new windmill appears.
  • Glowing power gears keep spinning their neighbours 15 s after disconnect.
  • Connect the mill for +100. Out of gears = game over.
